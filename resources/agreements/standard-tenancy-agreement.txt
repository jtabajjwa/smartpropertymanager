8. THE TENANT'S OBLIGATIONS

The Tenant shall:
   (a) pay the rent and other charges on time;
   (b) keep the interior of the Premises, its fixtures and fittings clean and in good condition, fair wear and tear excepted;
   (c) carry out minor day-to-day repairs (such as replacing bulbs, fuses and tap washers) and unblock drains blocked by the Tenant's use;
   (d) report promptly to the Landlord any damage, leak or defect that the Landlord is responsible for repairing;
   (e) not make any structural alteration or addition, paint in a different colour, or fix any satellite dish or antenna, without the Landlord's written consent;
   (f) not sublet, assign, share or part with possession of the Premises or any part of it without the Landlord's written consent;
   (g) make good any damage caused by the Tenant, the Tenant's household or visitors;
   (h) follow the estate rules issued by the Landlord from time to time, provided they are reasonable and given to the Tenant in writing; and
   (i) at the end of the tenancy, hand back the Premises in the same condition as at the start, fair wear and tear excepted, together with all keys.


9. THE LANDLORD'S OBLIGATIONS

The Landlord shall:
   (a) allow the Tenant quiet and peaceful enjoyment of the Premises for as long as the Tenant keeps to this Agreement;
   (b) keep the structure and exterior of the Premises (roof, walls, foundations, external doors and windows) and the main water, drainage and electrical installations in good repair;
   (c) keep the shared areas of {{estate_name}} reasonably clean, lit and maintained;
   (d) carry out repairs for which the Landlord is responsible within a reasonable time of being notified;
   (e) pay all land rates, ground rent and taxes payable by the owner of the property; and
   (f) issue receipts for all payments received from the Tenant.


10. ACCESS AND INSPECTION

10.1 The Landlord or the Landlord's agents may enter the Premises at reasonable times to inspect them or carry out repairs, after giving the Tenant at least twenty-four (24) hours' notice.

10.2 In an emergency (such as fire, flooding or a burst pipe) the Landlord may enter without notice, and shall inform the Tenant as soon as possible afterwards.

10.3 During the last [thirty (30)] days of the tenancy, the Tenant shall allow prospective tenants to view the Premises at reasonable times, with notice.


11. DAMAGE AND INSURANCE

11.1 The Landlord shall insure the building. The Tenant is responsible for insuring the Tenant's own belongings.

11.2 If the Premises are damaged so that they cannot be lived in, through no fault of the Tenant, rent shall stop being payable until they are made fit again, or either Party may end the tenancy by written notice.


12. ENDING THE TENANCY

12.1 Either Party may end a periodic (Ongoing) tenancy by giving the other at least [one (1) full rent period's] written notice, ending on the last day of a rent period.

12.2 A fixed-term tenancy ends on its end date. The Tenant may end it early by giving [two (2) months'] written notice [and paying ________], or by finding a replacement tenant acceptable to the Landlord.

12.3 If either Party breaches this Agreement, the other may give written notice describing the breach and requiring it to be put right within fourteen (14) days. If it is not put right within that time, the Party giving notice may end the tenancy by further written notice, in accordance with the law.

12.4 The Landlord shall not evict the Tenant, cut off utilities, remove the Tenant's belongings or lock the Tenant out except through the procedure provided by law.


13. HANDOVER AT THE END OF THE TENANCY

13.1 Before the Tenant leaves, the Parties shall jointly inspect the Premises against the Schedule.

13.2 The Tenant shall remove all of the Tenant's belongings, leave the Premises clean, settle all outstanding utility bills and return all keys, access cards and remote controls.


14. NOTICES

14.1 Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered by hand, sent to the email address or telephone number (by SMS or WhatsApp) stated at the top of this Agreement, or to any other address a Party gives in writing.

14.2 Invoices, receipts and payment reminders sent by the Landlord through its property management system by SMS or email are valid written communication.


15. DISPUTES

15.1 The Parties shall first try to settle any dispute amicably through discussion.

15.2 If they cannot agree within fourteen (14) days, either Party may refer the dispute to mediation, the local council, or a court or tribunal with jurisdiction in Uganda.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of Uganda, including the Landlord and Tenant Act, 2022. Nothing in this Agreement takes away any right or protection that either Party has under that Act or any other law; if any clause conflicts with the law, the law prevails and the rest of this Agreement remains in force.
